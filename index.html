HOW IT WORKS / 02

Memory, without
the archaeology.

  1. 01

    1. Onboard it

    Brief Lore like a new hire: your role, your projects, your duties per project.

  2. 02

    2. It experiences your work

    Lore reads your team chats and meeting transcripts, routes everything to the right project, and folds it into a living brain: decisions, owners, open questions - with citations to the exact source.

  3. 03

    3. You just ask

    "What did we decide about X?" - answered with receipts. Daily digest of what moved and what needs your follow-up. Queryable from GitHub Copilot, Claude, or any MCP client.
